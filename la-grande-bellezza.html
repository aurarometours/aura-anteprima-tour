The route

Stop 01The Colosseum

We begin where Rome begins. The cart stops on the side almost nobody reaches on foot, so you get the arches with nothing in front of them.

02
Stop 02The Imperial Forums

Two thousand years of city laid end to end along a single street, seen from the road that runs above them rather than from the queue below.

Stop 03Piazza Venezia

The white marble of the Vittoriano and the balcony everyone recognises, then straight into the lanes on the other side of it.

Stop 04The Pantheon

The best preserved building of the ancient world, and the only place in Rome where the rain is supposed to come indoors.

05
Stop 05Piazza Navona

Bernini's fountain, the shape of a Roman stadium still under your feet, and the terraces that fill it at every hour of the day.

Stop 06The Trevi Fountain

The one everybody wants. We time this stop to the hour that makes it possible to actually see it, and to photograph it.

The order of the stops changes with traffic, events and the light. Your driver always takes the best route of that particular hour, and you can ask to stop anywhere along the way.

Common questions

Where does the tour start?
At the door of your hotel or apartment, anywhere in the centre of Rome. The driver waits outside; if the address is inside a pedestrian area we agree the nearest corner in advance and use the same one every time.
Is the cart private?
Yes. The whole cart is yours: only your group travels, and the price is for the cart, not per person.
Do we go inside the Colosseum or the Pantheon?
No. This is an outdoor tour: no tickets and no interiors are included, and we say so before you book rather than after. You see the monuments from the street, from the sides most people never reach.
Can children come?
Yes, and a child seat is free. Tell us the ages when you book so the right cart and the right seat are ready.
What happens if it rains?
The carts have a roof and side covers, so light rain is not a problem. If the weather makes the tour pointless we move it to another slot or refund it in full.
How far in advance should we book?
Same-day is often possible, but the good hours go first, especially at sunset. Booking is online with instant confirmation, and cancellation is free up to 24 hours before.
